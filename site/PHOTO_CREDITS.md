## Architectural hero

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for this redesign. The image is an illustrative architectural study, not a claim about a real personal location or experience.

Final website assets:

- `public/photos/architecture-480.webp`
- `public/photos/architecture-960.webp`
- `public/photos/architecture-1440.webp`

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: atmospheric architecture artwork for the hero of a dark editorial personal website. Create a photorealistic architectural study, a tall portrait 2:3 composition. Monumental austere charcoal concrete walls and one tall slim opening, raking warm ivory afternoon light making precise triangular planes and long diagonal shadows across stone. Refined subtle concrete grain, cinematic stillness, low-key muted contrast, almost monochrome warm gray with a restrained antique gold hint in the light. Strong abstract composition: one vertical wall to the right, a diagonal sunlit face through the middle, deep dark foreground. Inspired by minimalist architectural photography, credible materials, quiet contemplative atmosphere. No people, no text, no lettering, no logos, no chess, no symbolic power imagery, no furniture, no vegetation, no watermarks. This is an abstract illustrative architectural study, not a real personal location or experience. Dark edges suitable for a near-black #0B0D0F webpage.

The favicon and social-preview image are original vector-based graphic designs rendered by `scripts/prepare-editorial-assets.mjs`.
